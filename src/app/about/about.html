About

Gameplay

Node Creeper is a simple graph-based roguelike adventure game. Explore a network of connected locations, battle enemies, discover loot, and prepare for ever increasing challenges.

Procedrually generated maps and points of interest make no two games alike. Choose your path carefully, manage your equipment, and make the most of the resources you discover along the way.

How to Play

⚔

Explore

Reveal connected nodes to discover what lies ahead. Every node will display a skull and a number of hidden enemies that are connected to that node. While enemy node will show the distance to the exit node. Nodes can contain an enemy, loot, or be empty.

☠

Fight

Combat is done by clicking on an enemy. Thier health is displayed above thier icon and damage below. Everytime you attack an enemy they will deal damage to you. If your attack is a killing blow, no damage is dealt by the enemy.

◆

Loot

Find gold, weapons, armor, and other useful items. Decide what to keep, sell, or discard. Hidden merchants can buy your unsused items in exchange for gold.

♜

Survive

Find the exit without falling to enemies, death is permanent.
